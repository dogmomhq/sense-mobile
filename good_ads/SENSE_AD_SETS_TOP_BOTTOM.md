# SENSE — Production-Ready Ad Sets (Top / Bottom Layout)

Every ad = TOP (reaction/activity cam) + BOTTOM (organic viral animal clip → real UI → tap, clock stops → win burst synced to top's peak → exit screen). Specs written to be Veo-promptable as-is.

## SET A — "MAKING MONEY KNOWING ANIMALS" (the identity angle)
The hook is the sentence itself: your useless animal knowledge is worth cash. These open with the money claim, then prove it on camera.

**A1. "My degree finally pays."**
- TOP: gen-Z girl in a zoology hoodie at a messy desk, deadpan to camera: "4-year animal science degree… time to make it pay." Plays, taps instantly, and on the win she stands up and salutes her diploma on the wall. Caption: MAKING MONEY KNOWING ANIMALS.
- BOTTOM: organic phone clip — okapi at a backyard fence (looks fake, isn't). Options: OKAPI / ZEBRA / GIRAFFE / BONGO. Win syncs to the salute.
- *Why:* "useless degree finally useful" is prime relatable comedy; okapi is the ultimate "only experts know this" flex animal.

**A2. "Farm kid tax."**
- TOP: girl in a carhartt and braids leaning on a truck tailgate, chewing a straw: "city folk really out here guessing." One-glance tap without looking at the phone twice. Win = she tips her hat, fans three $100s.
- BOTTOM: shaky barn clip — Highland cow vs Yak vs Bison vs Musk Ox round. Win on the hat tip.
- *Why:* rural identity flex; the audience that grew up around animals is the exact depositor profile, and "farm kid tax" is a caption they'll repost.

**A3. "Paying rent with raccoon facts."**
- TOP: girl at a laptop covered in overdue-bill sticky notes, tired. Notification. She side-eyes camera: "raccoon facts about to pay the wifi bill." Plays. Win = she peels ONE sticky note off and crumples it, slow grin.
- BOTTOM: doorbell-cam raccoon carrying a whole pizza slice up a porch railing. RACCOON / TANUKI / RINGTAIL / COATI (evil options — comment bait).
- *Why:* cost-of-living relatability + tiny-victory storytelling; peeling the bill note is the loopable payoff.

**A4. "The trivia hustler."**
- TOP: girl in sunglasses at night, streamer RGB behind her, stacking it like a poker montage — three rapid wins jump-cut, balance climbing each time, blows on her fingertip like a smoking gun.
- BOTTOM: three-round animal montage cut to the beat (serval → tapir → shoebill), each lock faster than the last.
- *Why:* poker-hustler grammar applied to trivia = "this is a skill you can grind," the exact positioning the compliance memo wants (skill, not luck).

**A5. "Smartest in the group chat."**
- TOP: girl screen-records a group-chat voice call — friends yelling wrong answers ("it's a llama!!") while she calmly taps the right one. Win = she unmutes: "that's why I'M the one getting paid." Friends erupt.
- BOTTOM: alpaca vs llama vs vicuña vs guanaco — THE eternal argument round. Win on her unmute line.
- *Why:* the ad literally performs its own comment section; alpaca/llama discourse is undefeated engagement bait.

## SET B — ABSURD ACTIVITY UP TOP (max wow reactions)
Top is someone playing mid-absurd-activity; the activity peaks exactly on the win. Bottom stays hyper-organic animal chaos.

**B1. Haircut Win.**
- TOP: girl mid-haircut in the salon chair, foils in, plays under the cape. Win = she jumps up screaming, stylist frozen with scissors, cape flying.
- BOTTOM: deer that walked into a barbershop (mirror multiplies it — looks like three deer). DEER / ELK / ANTELOPE / PRONGHORN.
- *Why:* forbidden-movement comedy (you CANNOT jump mid-haircut); top-bottom mirror gag (salon/barbershop) rewards rewatch.

**B2. Rollercoaster Round.**
- TOP: front-row coaster POV cam, her hair vertical, answering during the climb — tap at the crest — win screen erupts exactly as the drop hits and she screams for two reasons at once.
- BOTTOM: emu chasing a golf cart across a course, filmed from the cart.
- *Why:* the drop IS the win burst — the most literal "max wow sync" physically possible.

**B3. Wedding Dress Fitting.**
- TOP: bride on the fitting pedestal, mom crying nearby — phone buzzes in the dress pocket. She answers mid-twirl. Win = bouquet-toss celebration with the veil, mom crying harder for the wrong reason.
- BOTTOM: peacock that crashed an outdoor wedding, tail fully fanned next to the cake. PEACOCK / PHEASANT / TURKEY / PEAFOWL(!) — troll option.
- *Why:* sacred-moment interruption is elite absurdity; "peafowl" bait makes the comments a bloodbath.

**B4. Ice Bath Answer.**
- TOP: girl neck-deep in an ice bath, teeth chattering, breath fog — trembling finger hovers… tap. Win = she ROCKETS out of the tub screaming steam, towel guy panics.
- BOTTOM: polar-plunge walrus hauled out on someone's dock crushing a kayak. WALRUS / SEA LION / ELEPHANT SEAL / MANATEE.
- *Why:* cold-plunge content is huge; the temperature match between halves (ice girl / arctic animal) is the detail-comment magnet.

**B5. Driving Test.**
- TOP: girl in the driver's seat during her DMV road test, examiner with clipboard beside her. Phone buzzes in the cupholder. One micro-glance… tap at the red light. Win = silent scream with her cheeks puffed, examiner writing notes. Caption: "worth it."
- BOTTOM: turkey holding up an intersection, strutting between cars, honking everywhere.
- *Why:* do-NOT-do-this tension plus the suppressed reaction — a reaction ad where the comedy is that she CAN'T react.

**B6. Skydive Rematch (sequel to the proven one).**
- TOP: same skydive girl, now MID-PLANE-DOOR, instructor counting down — she holds up one finger ("wait"), answers the round, wins, and BACKFLIPS out of the plane on the win burst.
- BOTTOM: mountain goat standing on an impossibly sheer cliff face, filmed from a hiking trail. MOUNTAIN GOAT / IBEX / BIGHORN / CHAMOIS.

**B7. Violin Recital.**
- TOP: girl on stage mid-recital, phone lights up on the music stand. She keeps bowing ONE-HANDED, taps with the other. Win = she finishes the piece with a dramatic flourish exactly on the burst, crowd applauds — they think it's for the music.
- BOTTOM: bull that wandered into a quinceañera tent, wearing streamers, weirdly calm. BULL / OX / STEER / WATER BUFFALO.

**B8. Deep Squat PR.**
- TOP: girl mid-max-squat, spotter yelling — phone propped on the rack lights up. She answers AT THE BOTTOM of the squat, stands it up ON the win burst, racks it, double-bicep to the camera.
- BOTTOM: grizzly using a fallen tree as a scratching post like it's leg day. GRIZZLY / BLACK BEAR / KODIAK / BROWN BEAR.
- *Why:* the lift completing = the win completing is the cleanest strength-sync ever; GymTok × animal facts is an untouched crossover.

**B9. Sleep Paralysis Fake-Out.**
- TOP: night-vision style cam, girl "asleep" — phone glows, one eye opens. She answers without lifting her head off the pillow, wins, whispers "still undefeated," eye closes. Zero other movement.
- BOTTOM: opossum in the pantry frozen mid-heist, playing dead when the light hits. OPOSSUM / POSSUM / RAT / ARMADILLO (opossum vs possum bait).
- *Why:* minimal-reaction subversion — after 19 screaming ads, the whisper wins; "playing dead" mirrored in both halves.

**B10. The Getaway Scooter.**
- TOP: girl on an electric scooter rolling slowly through a parking garage, helmet cam angle — answers at a stop, wins, and does a full victory lap with one fist up, engine-revving her own "vroom" sounds.
- BOTTOM: escaped zoo capybara trotting down a suburban sidewalk chased (slowly, hopelessly) by one security guard. CAPYBARA / BEAVER / NUTRIA / GROUNDHOG.
- *Why:* both halves are low-speed chases — the laziest chase scene in history, top and bottom, is the joke; capybara content has a guaranteed floor.

# UPDATE — FORMAT LAWS v3 (locked)

1. **NO SPOKEN LINES. EVER.** Reactions are cheers, screams, gasps, roars only. Kills lip-sync risk completely, works in every language, and Veo renders wordless screaming flawlessly. Any words live in captions.
2. **PERFECT-STILL FIRST FRAME.** Every ad opens on a thumbnail-grade insane still (frame one must work as a poster). Generated first, approved, then animated with first-frame lock.
3. **Casting:** male streamers allowed; if it's a girl, she's hot AND mid-something-crazy. No one sits on a couch.
4. Win sync law unchanged: top reaction peak = win burst = tap/cheer/impact, same frame.
